Navi Mumbai & Mumbai Security Deposit Refund Guide: What Can Landlords Deduct?

Moving out of a rental property in the MMR (Mumbai Metropolitan Region) can be stressful, especially when it comes to getting your security deposit back. Whether you are transitioning out of a 1 BHK in Nerul, upgrading to a 2 BHK in Sarsole, or navigating agreement terms finalized through platforms like NoBroker, understanding what a landlord can legally deduct is critical to protecting your money.

1. The Standard "Painting Charge" Rule

In Maharashtra, it is a widely accepted industry standard for landlords to deduct the equivalent of one month's rent for repainting and minor touch-ups when a tenant vacates. However, this must be explicitly stated in your Leave and License Agreement. If the clause is missing, you can legally contest this deduction.

2. Deep Cleaning and Maintenance Fees

Landlords expect the property to be returned in its original condition, factoring in normal wear and tear. If you leave behind heavy stains, damaged fixtures, or require the landlord to hire professional deep cleaning services, expect a deduction ranging from ₹1,500 to ₹3,000. Using our deposit deduction estimator above helps you budget for these exact scenarios before handing over the keys.

3. Notice Period Shortfalls

Most agreements dictate a 30-day or 60-day notice period. If you need to break the lease early and move out immediately, landlords are typically entitled to withhold rent for the remainder of the notice period directly from your deposit. Always double-check the lock-in period clauses in your NoBroker or privately drafted agreement to avoid surprise penalties.

Normal Wear and Tear vs. Property Damage

Under the Maharashtra Rent Control Act, tenants are not responsible for natural depreciation. Faded paint, minor scuffs on the floor, or loose door hinges fall under "wear and tear." Broken tiles, shattered window panes, or unauthorized structural changes are classified as damages, and the repair costs will be deducted from your final refund.
